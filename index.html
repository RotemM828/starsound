StarSound

Hear the night sky. Point your phone up; every star in view plays a note. Brighter stars are louder, blue stars are higher, red stars are lower. Use headphones.

Tap Talk (or anywhere on the screen), then ask, like "Find Jupiter", "What am I pointing at?", or "Is Saturn up?" Say "help" for everything you can ask.
