Hear the night sky.

Point your phone up and every star in view plays a note: brighter stars are louder, blue stars sound higher, red stars lower. Ask out loud to find planets, stars and constellations. Headphones help.

Built for blind and visually impaired stargazers. Works through clouds, and even in daylight.

Then ask a question, like: what am I pointing at, or, find Jupiter. You can also tap anywhere on the screen to talk.

Privacy and security

No account, no tracking, no ads. StarSound has no server and no analytics. Nothing about you is collected or sold.

Your location is used on this phone to calculate the sky, and is never sent anywhere.

Motion sensors tell the app where the phone points. That stays on this phone.

Your voice: the microphone is only on while you talk, or while hands-free mode is on. To understand speech, your phone's browser sends what you say to its own speech service (Google in Chrome, Apple in Safari). StarSound never records or stores it. Every voice command also has a button.

Your observing log and settings are saved only on this phone. You can erase the log here, or by saying "clear my log."
